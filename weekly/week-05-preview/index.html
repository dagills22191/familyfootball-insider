Five Starters on a Bye: The Unbeaten Team Owns Three of Them

Kansas City and Carolina are off this week, and at press time five of their players are still in starting lineups. Three belong to the only 4–0 team in the league. The other two include a quarterback this desk said would not be a question this week. Also: a tight end decision finally changed, a quarterback decision did not, and the desk picks against itself.

By The Insider Desk · Rosters, lineups and injury tags at press time, Wednesday, Oct. 7 · Records are 2026, four weeks of fourteen · Projections: FantasyPros, Week 5 PPR

Second straight Wednesday filing. The desk mentions this for the record and will stop mentioning it when it stops being surprising.

The week has one theme and one caveat. The theme is the bye: Kansas City and Carolina do not play, and a lineup that has not been opened since Sunday does not know that. The caveat is the injury list, which is long, and which the desk reports straight. Every tag below is as of press time and several will change by Friday. Thursday's Tampa Bay–Dallas game locks a few lineups early.
